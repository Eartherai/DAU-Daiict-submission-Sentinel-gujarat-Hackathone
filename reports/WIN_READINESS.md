  camera evaluation set before claiming a 50-camera government run.

  precision, recall, and OCR accuracy.
- Browser FPS/CPU/memory/network telemetry is not yet measured.
- BoT-SORT/OC-SORT/DeepSORT and YOLO26/PaddleOCR candidates were not installed
  without compatibility/licence verification.
